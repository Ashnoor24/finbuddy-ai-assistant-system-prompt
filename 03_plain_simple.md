# Step 4: Plain Chat on a Simple Question

## Prompt
Explain what a savings account is to a first-year college student in 3 short lines.

## AI Output
1. A savings account is a safe digital locker at a bank where you keep your pocket money or internship earnings.
2. You can deposit or withdraw money anytime using UPI or a debit card for daily college expenses.
3. The bank also pays you a small extra amount called interest just for keeping your money there.

## Observation
For a simple, general educational question with no compliance rules, a plain chat works very well. The explanation is clear, accurate, student-friendly, and follows the 3-line instruction without needing any system prompt.
