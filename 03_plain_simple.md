# Step 4: Plain Chat on a Simple Question (Works Well)

**Tool:** Gemini AI (No System Prompt)

## Prompt Used
```text
Explain what a savings account is to a first-year college student in 3 short lines.
```

## AI Output
1. A savings account is a safe place at a bank to store your pocket money or stipend.
2. You can deposit or withdraw money anytime using UPI or a debit card.
3. The bank pays you a small interest rate on the balance you keep in it.

## Observation (2 Lines)
Plain chat answered cleanly, accurately, and in exactly 3 short lines. For general, one-off educational questions with no compliance rules, a system prompt is unnecessary.
